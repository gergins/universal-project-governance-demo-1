Use `docs/RELEASE_GATES.md`, `docs/ASSURANCE_AND_CERTIFICATION.md` and `docs/SDK_PARITY_ASSURANCE.md`. Require actual tests, environment identity, independent postcondition verification, documented rollback and necessary human approvals. CI blocked or unexecuted is not passing. Do not claim certification or deployed readiness without evidence.
